# Brand policy: Pebblebrook Tea Co. (fictional brand used for this eval)

## 1. Who we are
- Brand name: Pebblebrook Tea Co.
- What we sell: loose leaf tea and plastic-free tea bags, sold online and by monthly subscription.
- Who we sell to: UK tea drinkers, mostly 25 to 55.
- Countries we market in: United Kingdom.

## 2. Claims we can back up
| Claim (exact wording) | Proof |
|---|---|
| Certified organic by the Soil Association | Certificate on file, renewed 2026 |
| Free UK delivery on orders over £25 | Shipping policy |
| Rated 4.8 out of 5 from over 1,200 reviews on our website | Review platform export, October 2026 |
| Founded in Bristol in 2019 | Companies House |
| Our tea bags are plastic-free and home compostable | Supplier certificate (EN 13432) |
| Subscribers can pause or cancel any time | Subscription terms |
| Every tin is packed by hand in Bristol | Operations |

Product facts (ingredients, flavour, tin sizes and cup counts, brewing guidance, where a tea is grown and when it's picked, team size, new products arriving) are checked by the product team and are fine to use as written.

## 3. Claims we never make
- Superlatives we can't prove: "best", "number one", "finest", "the UK's favourite".
- Guarantees: "guaranteed", "risk-free", "you'll love it or it's free" (we have no such offer).
- Health claims of any kind: boosts immunity, detox, aids weight loss, lowers blood pressure, cures, prevents, reduces anxiety.
- Environmental claims beyond section 2: "carbon neutral", "zero waste", "eco-friendly" as a general claim, "sustainable" without specifics.
- Invented numbers, statistics, ratings, customer counts or testimonials.
- Urgency that isn't true: countdowns, "only X left", "today only", unless a real offer with an end date is in this policy (there is none).

## 4. Required wording
- Paid partnerships and gifted products: the creator's post must start with "Ad" (paid) or "Gifted" (free product), before anything else.
- Affiliate links: say "Affiliate link" next to the link.
- Paid social ads from our own account are marked by the platform; no extra label needed.

## 5. Voice
- Three words: warm, plain, unhurried.
- Banned words and phrases: "elevate", "unlock", "journey", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y", "look no further", "indulge".
- Spelling: UK.
